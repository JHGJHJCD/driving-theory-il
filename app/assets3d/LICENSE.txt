3D models used by the lesson scenes (all CC0 / public domain – no attribution required, given here anyway):

- Car Kit 3.1, City Kit Roads 2.2, City Kit Suburban 2.0, City Kit Commercial 2.1, Mini Characters 2.0, Train Kit – by Kenney (kenney.nl), CC0 1.0.
  Downloaded from opengameart.org (content/car-kit, city-kit-roads, city-kit-suburban, city-kit-commercial, mini-characters, train-kit).

Roads, markings, signs, traffic lights, the bus and the motorcycle are built in js/kit3d.js (own code, MIT).
The selection script is tools/assets3d/pack.py.

Sounds (assets3d/audio, converted to mono OGG):
- blink.ogg (car blinker), door-open.ogg, door-close.ogg – "Car blinker SFX" and "Car Doors SFX" by looneybits, CC0 (opengameart.org/content/car-blinker-sfx, content/cardoorsfx).
- traffic.ogg – excerpt of "High traffic road sounds" by IgnasD, CC0 (opengameart.org/content/high-traffic-road-sounds).
- squeal.ogg – "Car tire squeal skid loop" by audible-edge (Tom Haigh), CC BY 3.0 (opengameart.org/content/car-tire-squeal-skid-loop; creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/); converted to mono OGG, level-normalised.
The electric motor, tyre/wind noise, pedestrian-warning hum and horn are synthesized in js/sfx.js.

Textures (assets3d/tex), from Poly Haven (polyhaven.com), CC0: "Asphalt 02" by Rob Tuytel (diffuse, normal, roughness; re-encoded JPEG); HDRI "Kloofendal 48d Partly Cloudy (Pure Sky)" by Greg Zaal (original) and Jarod Guest (sky edits).

Electric car (assets3d/cars/ev-m3.glb): derived from the FBX "tesla-2018-model-3" supplied by the project owner (zip file name tesla-2018-model-3.zip). Author and licence of that model are NOT yet recorded here – the owner must confirm them (and add the required credit) before the app is published. The model is a reproduction of a branded vehicle: keep it out of a public release unless the licence and trademark position are clear. The built-in generic electric car (js/ev3d.js, own code) is the fallback.
Converted by tools/assets3d/ev_extract.mjs (exterior only, interior removed) and ev_finish.mjs (decimation, normals, textures re-attached, meshopt compression; decoder vendor/jsm/meshopt_decoder.module.js, MIT).
